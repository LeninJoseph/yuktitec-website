The idea behind Yuktitec

Intelligence should make life simpler.

Inspired by the Sanskrit word Yukti (युक्ति) — intelligence, reasoning and ingenuity — Yuktitec is built around a straightforward belief: technology is at its best when it works naturally for people.

“The best technology doesn't add complexity. It makes better things possible, simply.”
